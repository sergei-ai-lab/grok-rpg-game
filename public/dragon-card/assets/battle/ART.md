# Arena background

`arena.webp` is original artwork generated with the built-in image generation
tool for this battle view. Optimized to 1200 × 800, 126,468 bytes. No external
music, video, fonts, or paid runtime services are used by the new screen.

Prompt: Cinematic realistic empty fantasy arena, low side-on angle, ancient
basalt ruins, stormy volcanic mountains, cool blue light on the left and ember
orange on the right, open center, broad dark obsidian floor in the lower
quarter, no creatures, characters, text, logo, cards or user interface.

Dragon placeholders are drawn as SVG wyvern silhouettes in `battlefield.mjs`.
Real transparent PNGs can replace them independently; see `sprites/README.md`.
