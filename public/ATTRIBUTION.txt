DRAGONVERSE
Playable build adapted from Auto Monster by SmallTeddy.
Code license: MIT (Copyright (c) 2024 SmallTeddy).
https://github.com/SmallTeddy/auto-monster

Temporary creature and item tiles: Dungeon Crawl 32x32 tiles.
License: CC0. Author/maintainer: Chris Hamons.
https://opengameart.org/content/dungeon-crawl-32x32-tiles

The six named dragons (Vorathion, Nyxarion, Kaelith, Verdraxis, Aurion, Umbraxis)
use those tiles only as placeholders. Photoreal DragonVerse portraits are not in this build.
See MISSING_ASSETS.md.

Duplicate-copy leveling (1→10 via duplicate summons) is not in this build.
Training already raises a dragon through 10 ranks. That system was kept on purpose.
